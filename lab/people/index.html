People in our rooms

The harness for Photonic's people (M8, R60). It loads a population module into one of our real places. The population sits behind the interface we agreed (design/people/photonic-brief.md §3), and our checks run on every person in it.

Until Photonic's modules drop into src/people/, the reference population stands in: capsules with a nose to show which way they face. It walks our routes under our walking rules and steps aside for others and for you.

Your module

A name loads src/people/<name>.js; a path (with a slash) loads that file. The module exports makePeople(THREE, { ctx }).

What the page gives a population

  • ctx.walk: floorOf(h) and walkFor(half), our walker for a body of that half-width (src/make/walk.js). src/people/route.js finds routes with it (seek runs in slices within a frame's budget) and maps where can be reached (regions).
  • ctx.claims: the house's claim grid with a moving-bodies layer (src/people/bodies.js): hold, release, bodyAt, doorAt. Your own walk reads it too.
  • ctx.place.roomAt, ctx.works (the same calls the player makes), ctx.device.share (?share=).
  • From the page: show(rooms) as rooms come into view; hit for aiming, weighed against things by distance; tick every frame.

Checks

In the page's console, __peopleCheck() lists every finding (src/people/checks.js):

  • inside: standing where the house refuses a body that wide;
  • floats or sinks: more than 2 cm off the floor;
  • door: standing in a doorway the person doesn't fit;
  • overlap: two bodies, or a body and you, in one place.

The suite runs the checks on every set-up (tests/playwright/people.spec.mjs). __peopleLog holds the last 400 events.
