## Grass: Grassworks reached into (your words: "reach deep into this … good deployable grass which we need")

`design/references/grassworks/` holds:
- the demo, measured;
- what its public docs disclose;
- 16 open and published grass sources;
- our own grass design for R69.

Grassworks is $100 with source, under a commercial licence, and its code is obfuscated, so we took ideas only. The demo is beautiful but draws 0.8–1.7 M triangles a frame, past what a phone can hold. Our design is aimed at about 2 ms of a phone's frame, deterministic from the ground generator, and downloads nothing.

**Two questions are on your board for 7:30:**
- **g640c2a:** buy it or build our own? Default: build our own.
- **gdb4075:** grass straight after M7, ahead of M8? Default: yes.

**Later the same night:** the colony's Grassworks report arrived. That project took Grassworks apart at your direction and measured it. Our design held up. I changed five things it showed were wrong or missing:
- **The repeating pattern:** the 4 m tile of blade points is now turned or mirrored by position, so the layout doesn't repeat every 4 m.
- **Distant grass stays full:** when only 1 blade in 4 is kept, each survivor gets 4× the area, not 1.8×.
- **Footprints cost almost nothing:** the trail's fade is worked out on the GPU, so nothing sweeps the whole map each frame (that sweep costs Grassworks 1.6 ms a frame).
- **Cheaper shading:** most of grass's GPU cost is shading, not triangles, so grass gets its own small lighting function rather than the full standard material.
- **Matching light:** grass takes its ambient light from the same sky or probe as the ground.

The budget stays a guess until R50's lab measures grass at walking height. Notes for M8's people are in `design/people/prior-art.md`. This supports building our own grass (g640c2a).
