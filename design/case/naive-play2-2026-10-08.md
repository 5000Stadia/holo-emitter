# R59 stand-in: the naive bot as a first-time phone player, six seeds (2026-10-08)

**Question.** Until Kabe walks it himself, can a player who knows nothing of the solution finish case-1660 on a phone, in how long, and where does it stall?

**Short answer.**
- **All six runs solved**, in 101–148 turns.
- **26.5–41.1 estimated person-minutes** (median 34.6), inside R59's 30–60 band.
- **0 model calls** in every run.
- Wrong tries 0–3, mostly 1 (13 this morning, 5 after the first fixes).
- **Reloading mid-case resumed** the clues, notebook and room in all six runs. So did a **simulated browser crash**.
- **The worst stall is the muniment table.** 4 of 6 runs first took "the key" and got the steward's padlock key, out of the *closed* table drawer. 3 of 6 never learned the key's cut cord, although two thoughts pointed at it.

## How it played

The bot is `tests/.naive-play.mjs`. It runs on the real page at `localhost:8794`, 390×844, touch, WebGPU, `&voice=0`. It plays only from what the screen shows, as in the first report (`naive-play-2026-10-08.md`). New tonight:

- **Reaching in.** When an opened thing says "In X: …" and the hand offers "take out …", it reaches in. It steps up to the thing and looks down into it if the hand isn't under the dot. Daniel's gold note and the draft were taken this way in all six runs.
- **Thoughts.** The bot asks for "A thought…" in the notebook when:
  - nothing has been learned for 14 turns;
  - a lead has stood open 40 turns;
  - or there is nothing left to follow.

  It follows the thought at once: people the thought names first, then the lead's place. It opens the notebook from a conversation's header, because that is the only place it can be opened (fix 2 below).
- **Typing.** Before raising a matter, the bot sometimes types it in its own words instead:
  - odd seeds 15% of the time, even seeds 60%;
  - each line is a phrasing for that person and matter from `tests/fixtures/intent-corpus-1660.json`;
  - only phrasings whose long words the game has already shown are used, so nothing of the solution leaks.

  "They wait for you to be plainer" counts as **none**, and the bot then taps the chip. A line that didn't light the intended chip counts as **other**.
- **Press chips.** It presses chips the panel badges "press".
- **Reloading.** It reloads once, between turns 66 and 71. It checks the notebook, clues, persons, leads and Caught out, the room, the question count, and the "You take up the case again" line.
- **Crash restart.** If the browser crashes, it starts a new one with the saved case. That is what a phone's storage would keep, mirrored by the bot each turn. Seed 3 killed its browser at turn 92 on purpose (`--crash-at 92`) and resumed: 27 clues before, 27 after, same leads.
- **Seeds.** Each seed varies the order of chips, which lead it follows first, the sweep angles, and the hand (chips or typing).
- **Three earlier sets of six were discarded,** each time because the bot was less fair than a person:
  1. It followed only the first place a thought named. It also re-asked people on old leads before ever seeing the room where he lay: seed 1 never entered it in 147 turns.
  2. It ignored the "press" badge on chips, and dropped a thought it couldn't act on at once.
  3. It went to the clerk before Dame Anne when a thought said "press Dame Anne". That had made key_by_hand look unclosable.

  All of these were bot faults, now fixed. The table below is the fourth set.

1. **The muniment table: 3 of 6 never got the key's cut cord, and 4 of 6 took the wrong key first.**
   - The key and the drawer's padlock key sit 12 cm apart, one above the other.
   - From the table's front, an aim 6° beside Mr Hollins's key reads "take the key". A tap takes the steward's padlock key, from inside the drawer, with the drawer still shut (fix 1).
   - The player now "has the key". Then opening the drawer says "In the muniment table: a small padlock key", which is already in their pocket.
   - The thoughts "The key lies on the table under the east window: look at it closely, at its bow" (6 runs) and "Tap the key on the muniment table…" (1) sent the bot back. It still missed the key in seeds 1, 2 and 4: the small thing on the table again (playtest 2's C4).
   - Without the cut cord, key_by_hand can't close. The game still said "Enough to lay before the coroner", and the lock blank was still answered from Dame Anne's "the key-cord cut at his girdle".
   - Where the cut cord was found (seeds 3, 5, 6), the thought "With the key's cut cord learned, press Dame Anne on This morning at the door" closed key_by_hand 12–26 turns later each time.
2. **The middle hour of questioning, turns 60–105.** Every run had one or two stretches of 12–26 turns (2.6–6.9 person-minutes) with nothing new, all in the same pattern:
   - pressing Daniel or Francis on matters already pressed;
   - shows that get "I've told your Worship all I know";
   - meanwhile, the next real step was a place: the engrossment in the great chamber, or the garret chest.

   These stretches ended on a thought, or on a servant's word ("Daniel's run up the back stair"). This is where a person would put the phone down. The thoughts were asked 31 times in all; each one first needed a conversation opened (fix 2).
3. **The room where he lay waits 23–56 turns.** It is the scene of the death, but the fourth of the five leads open at arrival. The way there (great stair, best bedchamber, closet, Daniel at the door) took the "see_room" thought in two runs.
4. **The garret.** fled stayed open 23–75 turns. The reach-in now works every time: the gold note and the draft were taken in 6 of 6 runs.
   - From where the chest was opened (about 2.5 m), the "take out" hand showed only in a narrow window, about 10° by 10°. At 0.9 m it is easy (fix 4).
   - The first chest opened in the west servants' chamber is the maid's, and still says nothing (playtest 2's C7).
5. **Reload.** Clean in 6 of 6 runs.
   - In seed 4 the bot had walked 4.7 m within the servants' chamber since its last turn. The reload put it back where it last acted, and the case clock went back from 2.6 to 2.3 minutes (fix 6).
   - A probe found that an open chest stays open after a reload, but its "take out" hand is gone until you shut it and open it again (fix 3).

## Remaining fixes, ranked (game, not bot)

1. **A shut drawer's contents can be aimed and taken, under a cue that doesn't name them.**
   - **Repro:**
     1. Muniment room. Stand at the front of the east-window table, about 0.7 m from the key, on the room's side (camera at plan (59.02, −9.62) facing −y).
     2. Aim about 6° left of and 4° below Mr Hollins's key.
     3. The cue reads "take the key". Tap it.
     4. "You take the key." The case now holds `key_chest_steward`, the padlock key `rel: "in"` desk1. The drawer is still shut.
     5. Opening the drawer then says "In the muniment table: a small padlock key."
   - **Causes:**
     - `take()` (`src/make/works.js:253`) checks only the thing's own `take.requires`, not whether its host is shut.
     - The aim (`lab/manor/index.html:462` `aimOne`) tests only usable meshes (`near || works.meshes()`), so the shut table doesn't hide what's in it, despite the comment at `:447-448`.
     - The cue's words come from the kind's noun (`src/make/works.js:215` `take ${noun}`), "the key", for both keys.
   - **Fix:**
     - Don't offer or take a thing `in` a host whose opening is shut.
     - For a case thing, have the take cue use the case label, as the look cue already does (`index.html:691`): "take the steward's padlock key".
     - Filter the "In X: …" list (`src/make/case-play.js:73`) by `touched`.
2. **The notebook opens only from inside a conversation.**
   - Its button is in the talk sheet's head (`lab/ui/talk-panel.js:355`, `:368`), and `onOpenNotebook` is wired nowhere else (`lab/manor/index.html:351`).
   - A player walking the house can't see the Open leads or ask for "A thought…" without first tapping someone. Every one of the 31 thoughts tonight cost that detour.
   - **Fix:** a notebook button on the HUD, by the thumb's button.
3. **Reach-in is lost on reload.**
   - **Repro:**
     1. Open Daniel's chest. `reachIn('chest_daniel')` is `box_daniel`.
     2. Reload.
     3. The chest is still open (world doc), but `reachIn` is `null`, so there is no hand. The player must close the chest and open it again.
   - **Cause:** the snapshot (`src/make/case-play.js:255`) doesn't keep `openNow`, `touched` or `toldInside` (`:55`), and `reachIn` (`:58`) needs `openNow`.
   - **Fix:** save the three, or rebuild `openNow` from the works state on resume.
4. **An opened chest is hard to reach into from where you opened it.**
   - At 2.5 m from Daniel's chest (inside the 2.6 m reach, `index.html:443`), the lid swings out of the aim. The hand shows only around yaw +10°, pitch −25°. At 0.9 m, any aim at the chest works.
   - **Fix:** while a thing with `reachIn` is open, keep the hand on any aim within its bounds. Alternatively, open it with a half step toward it.
5. **A typed question that names another person reads as "What of <person>", not the matter.**
   - Repro with `readIntent` (`src/make/intent.js:191`) and this person's topics, early or late in the case:

     | To | Typed | Corpus label | Read as |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | Cressy | "Did Francis go to the buttery?" | the cut hand | `person:francis` |
     | Cressy | "How much did Francis lose?" | `cards` | `person:francis` |
     | Cressy | "Did you give money to Wragg?" | `gold` | `person:daniel` |
     | Francis | "Did anyone come down the great stair at half past ten?" | `person:daniel`, which holds "no one came down" | `whereabouts` |
     | Daniel | "Liar. The key was not by his hand." | `morning`/press | `keys`/press |
     | Dame Anne | "Did you go up to the muniment room?" | `whereabouts` | `none` |

   - 10 of 111 typed lines tonight went wrong this way: 9%, against the corpus check's held-out 90%.
   - **Fix:** when a matter's own word is present ("buttery", "lose", "money"), let it outweigh a named person.
6. **Where you stood and the case clock are saved only on a turn.**
   - `save()` runs after a step (`src/make/case-play.js:162`, `:261`). The position is read when it runs (`lab/manor/index.html:356`).
   - Walk after your last question and reload, and you come back where you asked, with the clock set back. Seed 4: 4.7 m and 0.3 min.
   - **Fix:** also persist on `pagehide` and `visibilitychange`. iOS kills tabs exactly then.
7. **The "when" answer shares one word with its proof.**
   - The true option, "staying on in the muniment room past eleven", is proved by:
     - "Cressy saw Daniel come down at twenty past eleven";
     - "Francis saw Daniel in the screens passage at half past eleven".
   - Neither says "muniment room" or "stayed". The lie, now struck, still shares "great stair" and "half past ten" with three live clues.
   - **Fix:** have Cressy's clue read "…come down from his master's rooms at twenty past eleven", or similar.
8. **The maid's chest in the west servants' chamber opens silently** (playtest 2's C7, still there). It is the first of the two chests a player opens there.

## Limits of this stand-in

- **It moves by `__place`, not by walking.** The person-time table charges walks, and the floor surcharge charges stairs, but door leaves and the reach (playtest 2's C2 and C6) cost a person more than that.
- **It reads every line instantly.** The person table charges for reading, at a steady rate.
- **The accusation is word matching.** Its wrong tries overstate a thinking person's tries on *when* and *means*, and understate nothing.
- **It never mis-taps.** A person also hits the Talk button, which opens the Accusation (playtest 2's C1, if still there).
- **Typing uses the corpus's phrasings,** filtered to words the game has shown. A person's own phrasings will differ.
- **The run files** are `run-1.json` to `run-6.json`, with the log, the leads' lives, the thoughts, the stalls, and the reload and restart checks. They are in the scratchpad (`…/scratchpad/n2/final/`).
- **The intent repro script** is `…/scratchpad/n2/intent-repro.mjs`.
