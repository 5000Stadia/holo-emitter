## 1. Bugs and friction, ranked by how much they hurt play

### Blocks progress: a designed clue cannot be had, or only by luck

**B1. Mr Hollins's key is hidden inside the candlestick's foot.** Still holds on B.
- **Repro:**
  1. Enter the muniment room.
  2. The beat says "A table under the east window with a key lying on it".
  3. Look at the table.
- **Seen:**
  - A book, the table candlestick and a lit decorative candlestick. No key anywhere (`pt/42-table.png`, `pt/45-candle.png`, `pt/97-table-now.png`).
  - World boxes:
    - `key_steward`: x 59.01–59.05, z 10.26–10.37, y 4.71.
    - `desk1_candle` foot: x 58.98–59.08, z 10.27–10.37, y 4.71.
  - The key lies wholly inside the candlestick's foot.
  - In run A I got it only by tapping bare table top beside the candlestick (`pt/47-key-tap.png`), where the forgiving aim ring happened to find it.
- **What it costs:**
  - `key_cut_cord` is lost. It is genuine for the lock.
  - Without it, Dame Anne's `anne_dawn` gate stays shut (it needs `key_cut_cord`), so the `lie_key` beat never fires.
  - The stall nudge (a809d436) sends the player to "the muniment key on the table", which cannot be seen.
- **Cause:** `lab/manor/index.html:288-292`. Every case thing built into a host's slot is set at `sl.at`. `key_steward` (slot `top`) and `desk1_candle` (no slot, so its kind's place slot, also `top`) land on the same point. Things sharing a slot need spreading, as the put-down code does with `0.05 * held().length` at :423.

**B2. A tap more than about 90 px from the screen's centre hits the wrong thing.** Still holds on B.
- **Repro:**
  1. Open the muniment table's drawer.
  2. Leave the view so the padlock key in the drawer sits low on the screen, about 180 px below centre.
  3. Tap the key.
- **Seen:**
  - The tap hits the table's drawer and shuts it. The key cannot be taken (`pt/48-drawer2.png`, `pt/49-drawer-down.png`).
  - With the same key 88 px above centre, the same tap takes it (run B).
  - Measured: the key's angle from the view axis was 0.338 rad, against a cone of 0.185 rad.
- **Cause:** `nearAim()` at `index.html:412` pre-filters the meshes to a cone round `camera.getWorldDirection()`, the screen centre, not round the tapped point. `aim(ndc)` then ray-casts the tap point only against that pre-filtered list, so anything off-centre is dropped. Tapping what you see is the phone's main verb; the cone should be built round the tap's ray.

**B3. The Asshover drawer shuts on the first tap and teaches nothing.** Still holds on B.
- **Repro:**
  1. Muniment room: aim at the north press, whose drawer "stands a hand open".
  2. Press the use button.
- **Seen:**
  - Run B: the drawer closes, nothing is said, no clue.
  - Only a second tap reopens it, and then the paper reader shows the memorandum (`pt/103-press-again.png`; the reader itself is good).
  - In run A, before ccde9545, the first tap gave the clue but also shut the drawer (`pt/56-press-tap.png`).
- **What it costs:** `drawer_asshover` gates `francis_saw_purse` and `cressy_cheat`. A player who sees the drawer shut on their tap moves on.
- **Cause:** the press's open drawer is toggled shut, and since ccde9545 "shutting a thing again" teaches nothing. The clue should come from looking at an open drawer, or the first act on an open drawer should read it rather than toggle it.

**B4. The body cannot be examined.** Still holds on B and C.
- **Repro:** Steward's chamber, first floor. Look at Mr Hollins under the sheet (`pt/111-body.png`).
- **Seen:**
  - No cue, no use button. `__aim` is null.
  - `body/laid-out` has no affordances, and its mesh is not in `works.meshes()`.
  - `__actOn(body)` says "It is not a thing you can carry.." (with a double full stop).
- **What it costs:** `body_wound` (genuine, means) and `body_cord` (since e2a00573 a third genuine route for the lock) cannot be had. The stall nudge sends players to "the body laid out in the steward's chamber".
- **Cause:** the kind has no "look" affordance. Since ccde9545 a refusal no longer teaches, so the old path (any act teaches) is gone too.

**B5. The chest's clean corner is no longer learnable without both padlock keys.** Still holds on B.
- **Repro:** Tap the locked iron-bound chest.
- **Seen:**
  - "The padlocks hold it shut." and no `chest_band_clean`.

## 4. Verdict

**Could Kabe finish this case on his phone today?** Yes, but mostly by reasoning past the evidence rather than through it.

**What works on a phone:**
- Arrival now faces Dame Anne.
- "Who is in the house" and the notebook's Open leads say where each person is.
- Tapping a person opens the panel.
- Chips and typed questions mostly read right (31 of 40).
- Press and show evidence break lies.
- The paper reader is good.
- The accusation, confirmed a group at a time, accepts the right answer.

**What stops him playing it as designed:**
1. **Key clues he cannot get.**
   - The muniment key is inside the candlestick (B1).
   - The open Asshover drawer shuts on his first tap and teaches nothing (B3).
   - The body cannot be examined (B4).
   - The chest's clean corner needs both padlock keys (B5).
   - Things tapped away from the screen's centre hit the wrong object (B2), which also traps the padlock key in the drawer.
   - Together these take out three genuine routes for the lock and two for the means, and the lead that opens Dame Anne's "empty hand".
2. **Show evidence on the wrong matter (C1).** Showing the key to Dame Anne without first asking "This morning" gets "I know nothing of that", and asking again repeats the denial (C2). He will conclude she knows nothing.
3. **The accusation gives no visible answer (C3).** After Confirm, nothing visible changes. The verdict and the confession are below the fold.
4. **He cannot tell the people apart (C4).** All four busts wear the same face, and the portrait view is narrow (C5).
5. **Performance on a phone.** A multi-second stall on first sight of the muniment room (C7), and about 540 MB of GPU memory.

**What to fix first:** B1, B2, B3, C1 and C3. Each is a small, local fix, and together they would make the designed path playable end to end by touch.
