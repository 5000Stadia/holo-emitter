### Confuses

**C1. The Talk button opens the Accusation on top of the person you wanted to talk to. Every time.**
- **Repro:**
  1. Arrive (or stand before anyone) so the thumb button shows the speech bubble.
  2. Press it.
- **Seen:** two sheets open. The person's panel opens, with "The Accusation" sheet over it ("Fill each blank…", five empty blanks). Reproduced 13 times out of 14, with holds of 40–120 ms (`pt2/005-talk-open.png`).
- **What it costs:**
  - It is the very first thing a new player does: the arrival puts Dame Anne in front of you with the bubble lit.
  - The first impression is "the game wants me to accuse already".
  - Every later Talk press costs an extra tap on the ✕.
- **Cause (confirmed by an event trace):** `pointerdown → pointerup → touchend → click: tp-st acc/Accuse`.
  - `actionButton` acts on `pointerdown` (`lab/touch.js:62`). `preventDefault` on pointerdown does not cancel the click that follows.
  - By the time the click is dispatched, the panel is open. Its Accuse stance button (`lab/ui/talk-panel.js:361-364`, at x 303–374, y 736–780) lies under the thumb, so the click lands on it.
- **Fix:** do one of these:
  - act on `click`/`pointerup`;
  - call `preventDefault()` on `touchend` of the button;
  - drop any click that lands within about 400 ms of the panel opening.

  Tapping a person on the canvas does not do this, because the 250 ms single-tap wait lets the click go by first.

**C2. A tap on anything beyond arm's reach (2.6 m) does nothing and says nothing. People included.**
- **Repro 1:** fresh case, arrival. Step back to about 4.6 m from Dame Anne and tap her. Nothing happens (`pt2/189-anne-fresh-aim.png`).
- **Repro 2:** garret. Tap Daniel from 3.3 m. Nothing happens.
- **Repro 3:** the closet. Tap the muniment door from across the room (`pt2/076-mun-door-tap.png`). Nothing happens.
- **What it costs:** each time I double-tapped to close in and tapped again, about 2 extra turns. A player will think the person is "not talkable" or the door is scenery.
- **Cause:**
  - `ray.far = 2.6` (`lab/manor/index.html:433`) bounds `aimOne`'s presence test too (`:447-451`).
  - `tapAt` only acts when `aim()` finds something (`:398`).
- **Fix:** a single tap on a person or a usable thing out of reach could walk you to them (`goTo`) and then act. Failing that, say "Too far: double-tap to go there."

**C3. At the gathering all four people stand on one spot. You see one figure (Dame Anne), and tapping her opens Daniel.**
- **Repro:**
  1. Reach "Enough to lay before the coroner. Sir Gervase has himself carried down to the great hall…" (`pt2/165-withdrawing-anne.png`).
  2. Go to the great hall.
- **Seen:**
  - Daniel, Cressy, Francis and Dame Anne all stand at (38.71, 5.62). A single projection shows: Dame Anne's (`pt2/166-hall-gather.png`, `pt2/167-hall-gather-near.png`).
  - Tapping her opens **Daniel Wragg**'s panel.
  - A player can neither find the others nor choose whom to accuse in person.
- **Cause:** `standIn(roomId)` (`lab/manor/index.html:323-333`) is deterministic per room: same spiral, same first clear point. Everyone sent to the same room lands on the same point. `presenceOf` (`:334-339`) calls it once per person with nothing to keep them apart.
- **Fix:** pass a taken-points list or a per-person offset (in a ring round the room centre, facing the player).

**C4. A small thing on usable furniture loses to the furniture: aiming at Mr Hollins's key opened the table's drawer.**
- **Repro:**
  1. Muniment room. Stand at the east-window table (`pt2/089-table-close.png`).
  2. The steward's key is a dark 7 cm sliver at the book's right edge, about 14 px across on screen. Tap about 14 px off its centre.
- **Seen:** "In the muniment table: a small padlock key." The drawer opens (`pt2/090-key-tap.png`). Only a tap on its exact centre took it (`pt2/091-key-tap2.png`).
- **Cause:** `aim()` (`lab/manor/index.html:454`) returns the direct ray's hit first. The forgiving rings run only when the direct hit finds nothing usable, and the table is usable (its drawer).
- **Fix:** when the direct hit is a host (a table, a press) and a case thing lies within the ring, prefer the case thing.

**C5. The chip row keeps its sideways scroll between people, so the newest, unasked chips sit hidden off the left.**
- **Repro:**
  1. Ask Dame Anne chips until the row has scrolled right.
  2. Close her panel and talk to Mr Cressy.
- **Seen:** his row opens showing "…ler Asshover | The game at tables | More…". "Who is in the house", "Last night", "The sale of Asshover" and "The engrossment" are all off screen to the left (`pt2/053-cressy-panel.png`). The same happened with Daniel (his first chip sat at x −778).
- **Cause:** `open()` (`lab/ui/talk-panel.js:656-660`) re-renders the chips but never resets `chipsEl.scrollLeft`. The Show drawer does reset its own row (`:461, :467, :483`).
- **Fix:** set `chipsEl.scrollLeft = 0` in `open()` and after each chip asked.

**C6. Open door leaves trap you. Double-tapping through a doorway can leave you nose-to-leaf in the dark.**
- **Repro 1:**
  1. Best bedchamber. Open the closet door (d20) with the thumb button (`pt2/068-closet-door-open.png`).
  2. Double-tap the dark opening.
  3. You stand against the open leaf, facing a black panel. The HUD still says BEST BEDCHAMBER (`pt2/069-closet.png`, `pt2/070-closet-try2.png`).
  4. It took 3 moves (back, strafe, turn) to get in.
- **Repro 2:**
  1. Great hall, east end. Open d13 to the great stair.
  2. Its leaf hides the opening from the hall's middle. It took about 6 looks and steps to find the way through (`pt2/029-east-door-open.png` … `pt2/037-d13-aim.png`).
- **Cause (likely):** `goTo` (`lab/manor/index.html:410-428`) takes the first thing the ray meets. An open leaf counts as a wall, so you are set "a step in front of it".
- **Fix:** let `goTo`'s ray pass through door leaves (or anything in `works` that is a door), as it already skips additive glow.

**C7. The first chest opened in the west servants' chamber is someone else's, and says nothing.**
- **Repro:**

### Reads wrong

- **Cause:** the group's `lead` is "; the murderer was with him by" (`design/case/case-1660.json:3991`).
- **Fix:** "; he was with him last, …" or "; the murderer had him alone by …". Alternatively, reword the options as times ("past eleven, staying on in the muniment room").

**R4. The Clues tab marks Dame Anne's true report as "broken".**
- "Dame Anne: Francis bloody on the stair at one" shows "broken by Cressy: Francis cut his hand in the buttery".
- She told the truth. What breaks is the suspicion of Francis, and his own "cut at supper".
- **Cause:** the clue is `effect: "false"` (a false trail; `case-1660.json:2857-2860`). `brokenBy` (`src/make/case-play.js:249`) strikes every false clue, and the panel prints "broken by" (`lab/ui/talk-panel.js:526`).
- **Fix:** for a false trail that is not a lie, say "explained by".

**R5. Francis's answer about Daniel comes with a narrator aside that solves the contradiction for you.**
- The aside: "Daniel Wragg said he came down the great stair at half past ten. Nobody at its foot saw him then, and at night there is no other way down."
- That is the inference the player should make. This is taste, not a bug, but it takes away one of the case's two best deductions.

## 4. Verdict

**Could Kabe finish it on his phone?**
- Yes. Every clue on the designed path is reachable by touch now.
- The leads point to the right person or place in the right order: Cressy → Daniel's lies → Francis → the garret → the gold → A.C.
- Shown evidence breaks the lies it should.
- The accusation offers only answers his clues name, and confirms a group at a time.
- I solved it first try, blind.

**How long?** About 35–45 minutes for a first-time player who reads everything. Add 5–10 minutes if he fights the doors and the reach the way I did. The house walk (hall to stair, the closet door, the back stair to the garret) is the slowest part, not the questioning.

**Top five things that would make it better:**
1. **Stop the Talk button opening the Accusation (C1).** It is a one-line fix and it is the first thing he will touch.
2. **Spread the people at the gathering (C3),** so the closing scene shows the household and a tap reaches the one you see.
3. **A tap on a person or thing out of reach walks you there and acts (C2).** Also let `goTo` pass through open door leaves (C6). Together these are most of the fiddliness.
4. **Prefer the small case thing over its host when aiming (C4),** and reset the chip row's scroll on open (C5). Both hide things the player is looking straight at.
5. **Remove the holodeck grid from the muniment doorway (L1), and fix the accusation's "was with him by" (R1).** One is the most broken-looking thing on screen in the case's key room. The other is the sentence he reads at the climax.
