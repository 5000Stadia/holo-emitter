# Plan dimensions: sourced numbers for a c.1570-1690 gentry house (research, 2026-10-06)

Purpose: real dimensions for the hybrid E-plan generator (R47). Nothing here is invented.
Every number is tagged:

- **S** = stated in the cited text (quoted or closely paraphrased).
- **D** = derived by me from a published plan with a scale bar (method in section 9). Accuracy about +-0.3 m.
- **U** = unverified: I only saw it in a search-engine summary or a secondary page, not the primary text. Treat as a pointer.

Conversions: 1 ft = 0.3048 m. Metric values are the originals when the source is metric.

Status of this file: written from my own reading. Two helper agents (Derbyshire houses; Belton / Thorpe / Chevening / Pratt) were
still running when the coordinator asked me to finish, so their findings are NOT merged here. Section 8 lists what is still missing.

---

## 1. What the evidence is, honestly

- Published room dimensions for these houses sit mostly in printed books (Girouard, Pratt's notebooks as edited by Gunther, RCHME, Pevsner, Country Life) that are lending-only or paywalled. I could not open them.
- Historic England listing text (historicengland.org.uk) returns HTTP 403 to automated fetch. Listing text below comes via mirrors or search extracts and carries bay counts and storeys only, never room sizes (listings do not give them).
- The best free numeric source turned out to be **English Heritage's phased plans** (February 2015, with scale bars) for Kirby Hall, Hardwick Old Hall and Bolsover. I measured those (D).
- Public-domain texts (Tipping *English Homes*, Gotch *Early Renaissance Architecture in England* 1901/1914, Gotch *Growth of the English House* 1909) give a handful of stated sizes.

## 8. What I could not find (and why)

- Storey heights ground / first / garret for a c.1660 gentry house, stair well and flight widths, door widths, sill heights: Pratt's notebooks (Gunther 1928, archive.org `architectureofsi0000prat` returns 401) and Girouard's *Hardwick Hall* (`hardwickhall0000giro`) are lending-only.
- Kitchen, buttery, pantry sizes: not stated anywhere I could open. Kirby's kitchen is in the cellar plan only; I did not measure it.
- Sudbury, Thorpe, Belton, Chevening, Groombridge, Tissington, Snitterton, North Lees, Eyam room sizes: no primary text opened by me. Assigned to the two helpers; their reports need merging into this file.
- Wollaton hall figures and Hardwick 25 ft / 26 ft ceiling figures are still search-extract level (U) and should be checked against Girouard or an NT guidebook before they set a rule.
- Historic England listings were blocked (403). If listing text for Sudbury, Eyam, Thorpe, Tissington is needed it must come via a mirror or a browser session.

## 9. Method for the D figures

1. Download the EH phased-plan PDF (public) and render at 300-400 dpi (`pdftoppm`).
2. Measure the printed scale bar in pixels (Kirby 10 m = 296 px at 300 dpi; Hardwick Old Hall 15 m = 323 px at 400 dpi; Bolsover Little Castle 10 m = 384 px at 300 dpi; Bolsover site sheet 25 m = 381.5 px at 220 dpi).
3. Find wall-to-wall distances by scanning pixel rows and columns across rooms for runs of drawn wall, and (for Bolsover) flood-filling a room from a seed and taking the bounding box; results that leaked through doorways were discarded or read by eye.
4. Report clear internal sizes, rounded. Expected error is about 0.3 m (one wall-line width plus one pixel of antialiasing). Pale-shaded (conjectural) walls make Hardwick Old Hall figures softer.
5. Scripts are in the scratchpad (`flood.py`, `dims.py`); no repo files other than this one were written.
